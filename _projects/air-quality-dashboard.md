The Air Quality Dashboard is an interactive web tool that collates and visualises data from air quality monitoring stations across Indian cities. The dashboard allows users to explore temporal and spatial trends in PM2.5, PM10, NOx and other pollutants, compare locations, and analyse the effects of policies and events on ambient air quality. Developed using Python, JavaScript and Leaflet, the dashboard aims to make air quality information accessible to researchers, policymakers and the general public.
