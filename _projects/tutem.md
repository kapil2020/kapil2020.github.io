TUTEM (Transportation and Urban Transportation Emissions Management) is a decision support system developed to integrate transportation planning with air-quality and emissions goals. It combines transport models, emission inventories, and dispersion modelling to evaluate the environmental impacts of different mobility scenarios. With visual dashboards and scenario analysis, TUTEM helps planners compare policies such as fleet electrification, route optimisation and demand management, supporting evidence-based decisions to reduce emissions while maintaining efficient urban mobility.
