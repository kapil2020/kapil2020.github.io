I am a Ph.D. candidate in <b>Transportation Engineering</b> at the
<a href="https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/" target="_blank"><b>Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur</b></a>, affiliated with the
<a href="https://www.mustlab.in/" target="_blank"><b>MUST Lab</b></a>.
I hold an M.Tech in <b>Transportation Engineering</b> from
<a href="https://www.iitr.ac.in/" target="_blank"><b>IIT Roorkee</b></a> and a B.Tech in <b>Electrical Engineering</b> with a specialization in
<b>Signal Processing and Machine Learning</b>. My research spans the intersection of <b>transportation systems</b>, <b>urban livability</b>, and
<b>environmental sustainability</b>, with an emphasis on integrating <b>AI/ML</b> into transport and health applications.

## Research themes
1. <b>Urban air quality & exposure</b> — portable monitoring, spatiotemporal modeling, and behavioral risk.
2. <b>Activity–travel behavior</b> — how awareness, health, and context shape mode choice under pollution.
3. <b>Transport emissions & sustainability</b> — road emissions modeling and low-carbon interventions.
4. <b>Equity in mobility & health</b> — gendered differences in travel, safety, and exposure.
5. <b>Machine learning for transportation</b> — prediction, optimization, and policy evaluation.

## Current work @ MUST Lab
- Discrete choice modeling (<i>MNL, LCCA</i>) with behavioral segmentation.  
- Spatiotemporal AQI analysis using GIS and statistical inference.  
- ML models for exposure and mode-choice prediction.  
- Urban informatics for sustainable, active travel planning.
